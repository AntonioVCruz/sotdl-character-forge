Interesting Things
Your character begins the game with one interesting thing. An interesting thing could be an unusual item, a character trait, an heirloom inherited from a mysterious ancestor, or something that makes your character unique. To determine your interesting thing, roll a d6 to see which Interesting Thing table you use. Then roll a d20 and consult the appropriate table.
